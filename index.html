Scientific evaluation and collaborative studies

Collaborative research project · 2026 Data collection · System integration & evaluation

Evidence-Grounded Scientific Originality Evaluation System

An evidence-grounded system for evaluating the originality of scientific papers through document screening, parallel agent review, comparative evidence, and traceable final assessment.

Data
Collected and curated evaluation sets spanning papers from five universities, CNS and highly cited benchmarks, and expert-reviewed samples.
Integration and evaluation
Integrated the end-to-end evaluation pipeline and assessed the complete system through benchmark discrimination, bibliometric correlation, and expert-agreement analyses.

Publication

Task-specific representations for cognitive NLP

Accepted poster, NLPCC 2026 Shared Tasks Sole first author

RepDuet at NLPCC 2026 Chinese BabyLM: Task-Specific Hybrid Representations for the Cognitive Track

Zhenhao Liu, Qiujie Xie, Minjun Zhu, Zhiyuan Ning, Yixuan Weng, Pinzhang Li, Jiajun Wang, and Yue Zhang

As sole first author, I designed task-specific hybrid representations for word- and sentence-level fMRI encoding, trained the models, and built controlled comparisons and stability analyses. I integrated DeepScientist into the end-to-end research workflow; after organizer reproduction, RepDuet ranked first in the Cognitive Track.

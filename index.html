Profile

Artificial Intelligence undergraduate at Westlake

I am an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at Westlake University and conduct research at the Westlake NLP Lab under the guidance of Prof. Yue Zhang.

I study how AI systems use literature, human guidance, and experimental feedback to drive scientific work. Alongside automated discovery with DeepScientist, I build portable workflows and evaluation pipelines that keep research outputs traceable and reproducible.

Research project

Evidence-grounded scientific originality evaluation

Ministry of Education of China project · 2026 Data curation · System integration & end-to-end evaluation

Evidence-Grounded Scientific Originality Evaluation System

Developed as part of a Ministry of Education of China project, this system evaluates the originality of scientific papers through document screening, dual-agent review, comparative evidence, and traceable final assessment.

Data curation
Collected and curated evaluation sets spanning papers from five universities, CNS and highly cited benchmarks, bibliometric records, and expert-reviewed samples for a six-level originality framework.
Integration & evaluation
Integrated the end-to-end evaluation pipeline across screening, parallel review, and evidence-linked rating, and assessed the system through benchmark discrimination, bibliometric correlation, and expert-agreement analyses.
